Our services
Coffee wholesale supply
We source and roast premium specialty coffee with a focus on sustainability, flavour, and consistency from farm to cup. Our blends, single origins, capsules, and drip bags give cafés and hospitality partners flexible, reliable coffee solutions that perform across different concepts and service setups.
Training & support
Led by certified Q Graders and experienced coffee professionals, our training covers espresso theory, filter coffee, latte art, and workflow — supported by ongoing calibration, troubleshooting, and preventative machine care to keep teams confident and coffee programs running smoothly.
Bakery wholesale supply
Fresh, handcrafted sourdough breads and pastries daily from our central bakery in Charoenkrung — viennoiserie, croissants, loaf cakes, tarts, cookies, and breakfast bakery staples built for consistency, quality, and ease of service across different operations.
Coffee machine solutions
Flexible commercial coffee machine solutions with loan, rental, and purchase options available. From installation and calibration to training and maintenance — reliable equipment and smooth operations so your team can serve consistent, high-quality coffee every day.
